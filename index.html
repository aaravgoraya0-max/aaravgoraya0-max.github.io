Climate Change: Myth or Reality?

Explore both sides respectfully, check the evidence, and cast your vote.

Overview

This page presents common arguments from both perspectives and links to authoritative sources so you can draw your own conclusions.

Arguments — Climate Change Is Real

  • Global surface temperatures have increased over the past century.
  • Multiple independent datasets and proxies show a long-term warming trend.
  • Observed impacts: sea-level rise, glacier retreat, and more frequent heat extremes.
  • Major scientific organizations attribute most recent warming to human activities.
  • IPCC
  • NASA Climate
  • NOAA Climate

Arguments — Climate Change Is a Myth

  • Climate has varied naturally in the past; skeptics argue current changes are within natural variability.
  • Short-term or local weather events are sometimes used to question global trends.
  • Some point to uncertainties in models or measurements.
  • Evaluate claims by checking data sources and peer-reviewed literature.
  • Read skeptic perspectives critically and compare against primary data.
  • Check methodology, sample sizes, and peer review status of claims.

Scientific Consensus

Most climate scientists and national science academies state that the recent warming trend is primarily driven by human activities like fossil fuel burning and land-use changes. For robust conclusions, prefer peer-reviewed literature and major assessment reports.
